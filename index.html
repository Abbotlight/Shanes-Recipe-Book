Keto Gummies

⏱ Prep: 20 mins ❄ Chill: 1+ hour 🍽 4 servings ⭐ Easy

Ingredients

  • 15 g 250 Bloom Gelatine
  • 65 ml Squash
  • 100 ml Water

Method

  1. Add the 15 g gelatine to the 100 ml cold water and leave it to bloom.
  2. Place the jug in a hot-water bath and gently warm it until the gelatine has completely dissolved.
  3. Add the 65 ml of squash and mix thoroughly.
  4. Pour the mixture into gummy moulds.
  5. Refrigerate for at least 1 hour.
  6. Remove gummies from mould, cut into 4 pieces and return to fridge uncovered until set.

Chicken Pizza Base

⏱ Prep: 20 mins 🔥 Cook: 30 mins 🍕 Makes 4 bases ⭐ Easy

Ingredients

  • 4 Raw chicken breast ~750g Shredded cooked chicken (target weight)
  • 150 g Parmesan cheese
  • 75 g Mozzarella cheese
  • 3 Medium eggs
  • 1.5 tsp Garlic granules
  • 1.5 tsp Italian herbs
  • To taste Black pepper
  • Additional Mozzarella cheese to act as sauce barrier

Method

  1. Cook all 4 chicken breasts in air fryer @ 180C for ~25 mins or until 75C.
  2. Blitz chicken in food processor.
  3. Remove chicken and drain as much moisture as possible.
  4. Add the chicken and the remaining ingredients to the food processor and blitz thoroughly.
  5. Divide the mixture into 4 equal portions.
  6. Shape one portion into a pizza base.
  7. Bake until cooked and browned on both sides.
  8. Add mozzarella liberally and cook for a further 2-3 mins, then spread and allow to cool.

Storage

Place 3 remaining portions into separate bags, flatten then freeze
